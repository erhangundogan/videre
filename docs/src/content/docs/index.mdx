## Why videre

Most photo apps want to *own* your library. They import everything into their
own storage, index it in a database only they can read, then nudge you toward
their cloud. videre works the other way round: it is a lens over a folder you
already own. Point it at a directory and you get a single SQLite file describing
what is there. Stop using it and your photos are exactly as they were.

- **It never touches your photos.** Nothing is moved, renamed, copied, or
  re-encoded. The one exception is [`videre fix-dates`](/commands/fix-dates/),
  which you run deliberately, and which only corrects a file's date.
- **Nothing leaves your machine.** No account, no upload, no telemetry. The one
  exception is [`videre search --location`](/commands/search/), which looks a
  place name up once and remembers it.
- **It will not delete anything behind your back.**
  [`videre dedupe`](/commands/dedupe/) prints what could go and stops. You
  decide, and you can review the candidates in a browser first.
- **Long jobs are safe to interrupt.** Preparing search or scanning faces can
  take hours on a large library. Ctrl-C is fine; rerunning continues from where
  it stopped.

Before you point it at a real library, read the [cautions](/start/cautions/).
It is short, and covers the handful of commands that do change something.
